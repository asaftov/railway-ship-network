# Ship Day: run of show

*A draft for the Ship Network concept. It is not an official Railway document.*

Ship Day is the first wave. Six paid creators and 15 to 20 community builders publish on the same day. One big launch day is easy to spot in deploys of the apps the videos build and in searches for Railway. Releases spread over several weeks blur together. Supabase did the same with more than 30 creators for Launch Week 6 and called it a Content Storm.

Ship Day is the first day of week 4. The schedule below counts from it.

## Who publishes

- **Paid creators (6):** Sonny Sangha, Codesistency, Jan Marshal, GreatStack, Leon van Zyl and Chris Raroque.
- **Community builders (15 to 20):** top template authors, active affiliates, Central Station regulars who post build write-ups and smaller channels that already make Railway tutorials. They get credits, swag and early access to the Deploy Bounty instead of a fee.
- **Railway's own accounts:** X, the changelog and Jake Cooper's account. Railway shares the creators' videos instead of running its own launch post.

Ship Day can land on the same day as a Railway product launch. Creators can feature the launch if they want to.

## Schedule

| When | Who | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Growth | Reach out to the six paid creators and confirm fees |
| Week 2 | Growth | Briefs signed, community builders invited and referral codes issued in Ship Station |
| Weeks 2 and 3 | Growth | Record a baseline: two weeks of first deploys by app type, branded search and mentions |
| Week 3 | Creators | Templates published. Growth deploys each one from a fresh account to test it |
| Two days before | Growth | Check each video's deploy segment for accuracy and confirm the publish time (16:00 UTC) |
| The day before | Growth | Open the bounty to community builders and queue Railway's posts |
| Ship Day at 16:00 UTC | Everyone | Publish |
| First two days | Growth | Check Ship Station every hour for template deploys, failed deploys and bounty entries. Answer creator questions and fix any broken template within 2 hours |
| The day after | Growth | Read the comments on every video |
| End of week 4 | Growth | First read: activated signups per creator, cost per activated signup, the before and after comparison and branded search. Change the wave 3 brief if a format is not working |
| Week 6 | Growth | Apply the stop rules, offer a paid series to the lowest-cost creators and open the bounty to everyone |

## What can go wrong

| Risk | What we do |
|---|---|
| A template breaks under load | Every template is tested from a fresh account in week 3. Growth is on call for 48 hours |
| Free VMs run out | Creators use `ssh railway.new` for their own build only. Viewers get the template link |
| Comments turn to the October incident | Creators were briefed with the facts. The comment rule pauses follow-ups and leaves fees alone |
| Bounty fraud spikes | Payouts wait until each deploy has run for a week. The bounty pauses if more than 15% of entries are flagged |
| A creator misses the date | Their video moves to Wave 2. Ship Day still has at least five paid creators and the community group |
